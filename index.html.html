Schrödinger's Crossword

Three squares in this grid (marked with a violet dot) are in superposition: each one accepts two different letters, and both make a real word Across and Down. Collapse all three one way and, read in grid order, they spell what is inside. Collapse them the other way and they spell what it is inside of. Either answer is correct; the puzzle only checks that the state is consistent.
